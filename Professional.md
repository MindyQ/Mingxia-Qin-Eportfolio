# Professional Bio

### Current Firm/Role


I have accumulated over a decade of rich teaching experience spanning international schools across Thailand and Shanghai, covering UK National Curriculum and IB PYP systems.
From August 2016 to August 2024, I worked as a Chinese & Homeroom Teacher at Shanghai United International School. My responsibilities included delivering Chinese and mathematics lessons, participating in English-medium co-teaching for science and thematic courses, managing homeroom affairs, and guiding Chinese drama and coding club CCAs.
Between August 2014 and June 2016, I served as a Chinese Teacher and Grade 1 Chinese Teaching Team Leader at Shanghai Hongqiao International School (IB PYP), collaborating on math and inquiry-based unit teaching in English.
Earlier from May 2013 to May 2013, I was a primary Chinese instructor at Thailand’s Wattanasuksa School, organizing cultural events for traditional Chinese festivals.
Additionally, from August 2025 to July 2026, I took up the post of Mathematics Teacher at Avenues World School, taking part in curriculum development and cross-grade interdisciplinary teaching work.
